I am a research collaborator in Computer Science at UC Davis, working with Professor Zhe Zhao. My research interests are human-centered AI, multimodal AI, and agentic systems.

I study how AI agents can simulate human perspectives to support creative work and help us understand social media. My recent work explores creator-directed video generation with simulated audience feedback, and the use of social media agents to study short-form video addiction.

I earned bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Statistics (Machine Learning Track) at UC Davis in 2026.

## Research

- **Human–AI co-creation:** CHIEF supports iterative video refinement through creator direction and feedback from simulated audiences.
- **Social media and well-being:** Social media agents help compare addiction-related tendencies and identify addictive features in short-form videos.
